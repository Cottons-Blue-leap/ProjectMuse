## Rationale

Composition is unambiguously public domain (Gossec d. 1829 → PD since 1899, worldwide).
The score is taken from the Wier 1918 *Masterpieces of Piano Music* anthology, a US
1918 publication that is itself public domain. We arrange from this PD piano setting.

⚠️ Branch noted (analogous to ⑭-prior Colonel Bogey's "River Kwai March" trap): the
*popular* concert version of this Gavotte is **Willy Burmester's 1904 violin
arrangement**. Burmester d. 1933 → that arrangement is also PD (life+70 = 2003), so
even that path is clean — but we do not rely on it; we work from the PD score above and
do not borrow Burmester-specific bowings/ornaments.
