# Source Art — Rights Notes (Salut d'Amour cover)

## Painting
- Artist: John William Waterhouse (1849-1917)
- Title: *The Soul of the Rose* (also known as *My Sweet Rose*)
- Year: 1903 · oil on canvas
- Inspiration: Tennyson's poem *Maud* — "Come into the garden, Maud… / And the soul of the rose went into my blood."
- Holding: private collection (sold at auction; not in a public museum)
- Subject: a young woman in profile leaning to breathe the scent of climbing pink roses against a sunlit Italianate wall — a single, absorbed, lyrical figure.

## Public Domain Status
- **Public domain (strong, worldwide)** — artist died 1917 → life+70 cleared 1987.
- US: published 1903 → PD.
- A faithful 2D photographic reproduction of a PD artwork carries no new copyright (PD reproduction of PD work). PD by age regardless of the painting's private ownership.
