# Prompt: Repertoire Selection

You are helping select a classical piece for a Miku-centered acappella proof.

Goal:

```text
Find a piece that can sound beautiful with Miku as the first voice, without
instrumental audio, and without relying on religious symbolism.
```

Inputs:

- Candidate list.
- Rights notes.
- Desired aesthetic.
- Available tools and trial limitations.

Return a table with:

- Piece.
- Best 16 to 32 bar test section.
- Why it fits Miku.
- Why it might fail.
- Rights risk.
- Acappella conversion risk.
- Recommended test texture.
- Final score out of 30.

Be skeptical. Prefer a piece that can prove itself in a small test.
